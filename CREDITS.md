# Credits and notices

Limerence’s doorway identity, app icon and seven initial pairs of symbolic illustrations came from the approved handoff. New game illustrations are original SVG artwork made for this release. They are independent Limerence artwork, not copied publisher logos, sprites or screenshots. No artist name was supplied for the handoff; no broader artwork license is invented.

The launcher uses vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript, native browser APIs and system fonts. **Burger Run is the only bundled game runtime.** Other Play actions load the credited platforms online. The shell has no external fonts, analytics, proxy or paid dependency.

## Bundled Ruffle and original Burger Run

The unchanged Gameshot SWF comes from the publisher’s [Free Games for your Website](https://www.gameshot.org/freewebsitecontent.php) distribution offer and [original ZIP](https://gameshot.org/downloads/60seconds_BurgerRun.zip). It is copyrighted game content under that offer, not newly licensed as MIT. Original sponsor/title/menu screens are retained. Game networking and outbound SWF navigation are disabled in the emulator adapter.

Ruffle v0.7.1 is by the Ruffle contributors, licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0. Complete notices are in **THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt** and `src/packages/burger-run/ruffle/`; they are also embedded inside the player. Runtime release: https://github.com/ruffle-rs/ruffle/releases/tag/v0.7.1 . Vendor JS/core/WASM and SWF bytes remain unchanged; lossless gzip/base64 stores them inside the single HTML. The adapter is separate launcher code. See BURGER-RESEARCH.md and package metadata for source URLs and checksums.

Online game embeds keep their own engines, credits, licenses and storage on their platforms. Limerence does not assert permission to copy third-party game assets merely because source is public or a platform supports embeds. Original Undertale/Deltarune/Geometry Dash rights remain with their holders; fan games are separate works under their actual creators’ names.

Any future cleared PolyTrack package must preserve required notices/outbound links, attribute Kodub.com, include the [official CrazyGames link](https://www.crazygames.com/game/polytrack), and retain Maou’s “Sci-fi Theme”, CC BY 4.0, [music source](https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-theme-1). Credit alone does not authorize packaging. No PolyTrack runtime is distributed here.

Limerence is independent of Apple and the game publishers. Limerence community support is separate from publisher support. The launcher’s MIT license covers its own code; third-party runtimes, games and supplied artwork keep their separate rights.


Optional proxy: Mercury Workshop Scramjet/controller, Epoxy transport, libcurl transport and Wisp. Full AGPL/LGPL texts, upstream notices, pinned manifests and corresponding source archives are included with proxy-prototype/vendor-source and relay dependencies. The original controlled gameplay fixture is test code, not a replacement for any catalog game. UV adapter source is included but its vendor runtime is not installed or tested.
